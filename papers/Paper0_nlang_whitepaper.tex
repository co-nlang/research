\begin{abstract}
n/ is a semantic operating system built on a stance: meaning is
\emph{discovered, not invented}; the programmer is an \emph{observer}. From that
stance two engineering decisions follow that the n/ paper series exists to back.
First, identity is content-addressable by \emph{relation}: a subspace is named by
the spectral fingerprint of its projection operator $P_A$ (the
\textbf{CAID}, Content Addressable Identifier; Yoneda's principle, an object is
its relations). Second, navigation is by \emph{geometric gravity} (``smell
search''), which demands that fingerprints carry \emph{phase}. The second
requirement is not free: through the EML completeness result and Sol\`er's
theorem it \emph{forces} the lattice's scalar field to be $\C$, hence forces n/
into Hilbert space --- quantization as geometric necessity, not style. Quantum
structure is non-commutative, so observation must proceed through commutative
\emph{contexts} (Bohrification), and Kochen--Specker then guarantees there is no
observer-independent global section. The obstruction-ladder series computes the
\emph{exact} structure and \emph{bound} of that contextuality. Its central output
is read by the specification itself (APP\_06 \S6.5) as the spectrum of
$\hbarn$ --- the number of trust-contexts needed to describe a value: the
\emph{resonance tower} is that spectrum, it is \emph{discrete}, and it has a
\emph{ceiling at $H^3$} (Paper~XXII). Underneath sits the master dichotomy: the
top class vanishes universally if and only if $n=4$. The specification poses the
question (content-addressing in a forced-quantum, contextual universe); the
papers answer it (the contextuality is a bounded, discrete ladder). This document
is the bridge between the two --- and, like every result it summarizes, it is
itself a bridge.
\end{abstract}

% =====================================================================
\section{Two engineering decisions (the spec side)}
% =====================================================================

The specification opens with a stance, not a feature list: \emph{writing programs
is discovery, not invention}. The programmer is an \emph{observer} of structure
that is already there. Two design decisions follow; the paper series is their
mathematical backing.

\paragraph{Decision 1 --- identity by relation (CAID).}
In n/ a subspace's identity is not its name but its projection feature in Hilbert
space (SPEC\_13 \S1). The \textbf{CAID} (Content Addressable Identifier) is the
intrinsic identifier: the spectral-geometric fingerprint of the projection
operator $P_A$, carrying both a collision-resistant particle digest and a
wave-like spectral sketch. Its justification is \emph{Yoneda's principle}: an
object is determined by its relations; the CAID encapsulates the interference of
$P_A$ with every other operator in the universe. Names (``observation aliases'')
are relative observer labels; the CAID is the absolute geometric coordinate.

\paragraph{Decision 2 --- navigation by geometric gravity (smell search).}
Discovery is \emph{gravity collapse}: declaring a dependency applies a geometric
positioning constraint, and the engine navigates the global lattice along the
gradient of ``semantic gravity'' (SPEC\_13 \S2, \S6; APP\_06 \S1). For two
fingerprints to attract --- to have a usable \emph{distance} before download ---
the fingerprint must carry \emph{phase}, not just a scalar hash.

\paragraph{Why the second decision forces quantum geometry.}
Phase is exactly what a real scalar cannot supply, and the consequences cascade
(APP\_06 \S2--\S4):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{EML completeness} --- a single binary operator
  $\mathrm{eml}(x,y)=\exp(x)-\ln(y)$ self-bootstraps the elementary functions,
  but only over $\C$ (it is fully holomorphic only there).
  \item \textbf{Sol\`er's theorem} --- an orthomodular lattice with an infinite
  orthogonal sequence has scalar field $\in\{\mathbb R,\C,\mathbb H\}$. n/'s
  negation $!$ makes the lattice orthomodular; EML forces the choice $\C$.
  \item Hence n/ is \emph{forced} into Hilbert space. ``Quantization is not a
  style but the geometric necessity of a lattice meeting EML completeness.''
\end{itemize}
Quantum structure is non-commutative. Bohrification (APP\_06 \S4) restores
readability by observing the non-commutative ontology through a family of
commutative subalgebras (contexts / perspectives); the projection operator $P_A$
recovered from those local sections \emph{is} the CAID. The cost is named by
Kochen--Specker: there is \emph{no} observer-independent global section, so an
observer is necessarily bound to a perspective and a horizon.

The hard question both decisions inherit is therefore:

\begin{quote}
\emph{In a forced-quantum, contextual universe, how badly does content-addressing
by observation fail to glue --- and is that failure bounded?}
\end{quote}

The obstruction-ladder series answers this. It is the part of n/'s mathematical
backing that carries the hardest problem, CAID.

\section{From semantics to symplectic geometry (the bridge)}
% =====================================================================

The papers work in the \emph{characteristic-2 shadow} of the spec's $\C$-Hilbert
geometry: the stabilizer / Pauli picture, $V=\F_2^{2n}$, where the contextuality
of stabilizer constructions (Mermin and beyond) is combinatorially exact. This
is the standard arena for contextuality proofs, and the dictionary that carries
the spec-side question into it is the following.

Two points fix the dictionary.

\textbf{Contexts are observation frames, and gluing is the whole question.} A
Lagrangian is a maximal set of observables seen at once (pairwise commuting); the
nerve records how frames overlap. A global section of the nerve would be the
observer-independent value table KS forbids. Everything downstream measures the
\emph{failure} of such gluing.

\textbf{$\hbarn$ is the depth of that failure, and it is what the ladder
computes.} The spec defines $\hbarn$ as the number of commutative contexts needed
to fully describe a value: $\hbarn^{\mathrm{eff}}\approx0$ when one context
suffices (classical), finite when several must be superposed (APP\_06 \S6.4).
Read through the dictionary, \emph{this is the arity of the obstruction}, and the
series turns it into a precise, discrete spectrum.

The base case is Bohrification itself (Paper~II): the original, $1$-truncated
bridge presenting a non-commutative structure by the diagram of its commutative
contexts. n/ computes the \emph{higher} gluing obstructions Bohrification left
implicit --- the series is, in one line, the \emph{cohomological completion of
Bohrification}, and the completion \emph{terminates}:

\begin{theorem}[Resonance ceiling; Paper~XXII]
For symmetric (bilinear, Pauli) data the obstruction tower stops at $H^3$: the
canonical degree-4 assembly is exact ($c=\delta a$), and there is no genuine
arity-5 invariant. Hence $\hbarn^{\mathrm{eff}}$ has a \emph{finite, third-order
ceiling}: no value ever requires its contextuality to be witnessed above $H^3$.
\end{theorem}

This is the spec's payoff made rigorous \emph{without} the Lohmiller--Slotine
route (whose ``exact'' reconstruction is challenged --- Vattay's comment shows it
is semiclassical/WKB; APP\_06 \S6 caveat). The rigorous anchor for $\hbarn$ is
Bohrification plus this series, not L--S.

\section{The master dichotomy: $n=4$}
% =====================================================================

Beneath the ceiling sits the sharpest result of the ladder.

\begin{theorem}[Master dichotomy; Papers~XVIII, XXI]
The top ($H^3$) obstruction vanishes universally,
\[
   [\,n_a\,]=[\delta\mu]=0 \ \text{for all configurations} \iff n=4 .
\]
For $n=3$ it vanishes only partially; for $n=4$ it is identically trivial; for
$n\ge5$ it is alive, carried by the modulus $\rad(\omega|_W)$ (Papers~XIX, XX).
\end{theorem}

In $\hbarn$ terms: at $n=4$ the third level of the spectrum is always empty ---
no value ever forces Borromean ($H^3$) contextuality --- so self-observation is
free of obstruction up to the ceiling. This is the precise sense in which $n=4$
is distinguished: it is the unique dimension where the otherwise-bottomless tower
of self-observation closes coherently at finite degree.

\section{The two faces are one}
\label{sec:onearena}
% =====================================================================

The specification and the series are two views of one claim. Three
identifications make it precise.

\textbf{``Discovery, not invention'' is realism about the observation-structure,
not about values.} Kochen--Specker forbids an observer-independent table of
values. So what is discovered-not-invented is not values but the \emph{structure
of observation} --- the contexts, the nerve, the obstruction tower. The realism
survives precisely by being relocated from values to observation-structure; and
the firewall that keeps this from floating is that every claim of ``essence'' is
cashed out as a class in $H^*$.

\textbf{Yoneda appears twice, and they are the same arrow.} CAID is Yoneda at the
level of identity (an object is its relations / its $P_A$-fingerprint); the
self-discovery protocol (Ouroboros / OODP) is Yoneda made reflexive (the universe
addressing its own contents). The obstruction tower measures when that reflexive
self-address is coherent.

\textbf{Observation has no bottom; the ceiling and $n=4$ are where it closes.}
Because the self-representation map is secondary (its primary form vanishes,
$f^*\omega=0$), fixing one global frame only spawns the next degree:
$\text{Bohr}\to\mu\to n_a\to\cdots$. This bottomless reflexivity is the honest
face of the $\infty$ in $\infty$-Yoneda --- ``building bridges forever'' is not a
failure to arrive but the signature of an $\infty$-categorical self-embedding,
where the bridge \emph{is} the content. The series shows the tower nonetheless
\emph{terminates} in two senses: a universal ceiling at $H^3$ (Paper~XXII), and a
unique dimension $n=4$ where even the top rung is always trivial.

\section{The honest frontier}
% =====================================================================

A whitepaper ends with what is not settled, at the right altitude.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Is the ceiling exotic-proof? (item 21).} Paper~XXII shows the
  \emph{natural} obstruction stops at $H^3$. Whether an \emph{exotic} arity-5
  invariant could furnish a new $H^4$ obstruction for $n\ge5$ is \emph{reduced},
  not closed: to ``Witt + polarization + a representability condition + finite
  verified computations,'' the unique degree-4 ambient invariant being the
  decomposable $q^2$. The single residual is a naturality/representability
  condition --- itself the statement that the framework's obstruction is the
  standard contextuality (value-assignment) obstruction.
  \item \textbf{The explicit bridge never finitely closes (item 23).} The
  identification $n_a=\langle\Sq^1\omega,[K_5]\rangle$ holds, but its join is a
  \emph{secondary} operation (Kudo transgression): no finite cochain formula. The
  logical ground does bottom out --- at the proven theorems of Kudo and Quillen
  --- but the explicit realization is rebuilt at every degree. This is the
  $\infty$-coherence of self-representation, not a gap.
\end{itemize}

Both open ends are the same kind: the perpetual, higher-coherence re-building of
the one bridge. That it never finitely finishes is the mathematical shape of
self-representation, not an incompleteness of the program.
