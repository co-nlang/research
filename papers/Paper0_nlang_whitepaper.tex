\begin{abstract}
n/ is a semantic operating system built on a stance: meaning is
\emph{discovered, not invented}; the programmer is an \emph{observer}. Two
engineering decisions follow, and the n/ paper series exists to give each a
mathematical backing. First, identity is content-addressable by \emph{relation}:
a subspace is named by the spectral fingerprint of its projection operator $P_A$
(the \textbf{CAID}, Content Addressable Identifier; Yoneda's principle, an object
is its relations). Second, navigation is by \emph{geometric gravity} (``smell
search''), which demands fingerprints carry \emph{phase}. The second requirement
is not free: an orthomodular observation logic restricts the scalar field to
$\{\RR,\C,\HH\}$ (Sol\`er; hypotheses flagged in \S1), and within that
classification non-trivial \emph{continuous} observation selects $\C$ ---
Paper~VI's pincer: $\RR$'s phase group is discrete, $\HH$'s is perfect,
annihilating the $d_2$ transgression that realizes contextuality --- with EML
completeness over $\C$ as an independent computational echo. So n/ is
\emph{forced} into Hilbert space, modulo two flagged hypotheses (\S1);
quantization is geometric necessity, not style. Quantum structure is non-commutative, so observation proceeds through
commutative \emph{contexts} (Bohrification), and Kochen--Specker then guarantees
there is no observer-independent global section. The obstruction-ladder series
computes the \emph{exact} structure and \emph{bound} of that contextuality; the
specification itself reads its central output (APP\_06 \S6.5) as the spectrum of
$\hbarn$, the number of trust-contexts needed to describe a value: the
\emph{resonance tower} is that spectrum, it is \emph{discrete}, and it has a
\emph{ceiling at $H^3$} (Paper~XXII). Beneath the ceiling, the top class vanishes
universally if and only if $n=4$ (Paper~XXI) --- exactly the dimension where n/'s
own self-description (the Ouroboros self-evolution loop) closes coherently. The
discovery engine spans the whole ladder; only the reflexive self-representation
selects $n=4$. The specification poses the question; the papers answer it. This
document is the bridge between them --- and, like every result it summarizes, it
is itself a bridge.
\end{abstract}

\section{Two engineering decisions (the spec side)}
% =====================================================================

The specification opens with a stance, not a feature list: \emph{writing programs
is discovery, not invention}. The programmer is an \emph{observer} of structure
that is already there. Two design decisions follow; the paper series is their
backing.

\paragraph{Decision 1 --- identity by relation (CAID).}
A subspace's identity is not its name but its projection feature in Hilbert space
(SPEC\_13 \S1). The \textbf{CAID} (Content Addressable Identifier) is the
intrinsic identifier: the spectral-geometric fingerprint of the projection
operator $P_A$, carrying both a collision-resistant particle digest and a
wave-like spectral sketch. Its justification is \emph{Yoneda's principle}: an
object is determined by its relations; the CAID encapsulates the interference of
$P_A$ with every other operator. Names (``observation aliases'') are relative
observer labels; the CAID is the absolute geometric coordinate.

\paragraph{Decision 2 --- navigation by geometric gravity (smell search).}
The genuine root of the program is a spectral-geometry problem: find a
permutation-invariant, collision-resistant fingerprint of a lattice element that
also decides geometric \emph{inclusion} --- via the Laplacian spectrum of its
Hasse diagram, with Weyl-law stability and Cauchy interlacing (the original
``smell search'' open problem). Discovery is then \emph{gravity collapse}:
declaring a dependency applies a positioning constraint and the engine navigates
along the gradient of semantic gravity (SPEC\_13 \S2, \S6). For two fingerprints
to attract --- to have a usable \emph{distance} before download --- the
fingerprint must carry \emph{phase}, not just a real scalar: a cryptographic hash
is collision-resistant but mutually \emph{orthogonal}, so two hashes reveal no
nearness without fetching both --- yet smell search needs nearness \emph{before}
fetching. Only \emph{interference} between spectral signatures supplies it
(overlapping spectra attract), and interference needs complex amplitudes; real
magnitudes do not interfere.

\paragraph{Why the second decision forces quantum geometry.}
Phase is exactly what a real scalar cannot supply, and the consequences cascade
(APP\_06 \S2--\S4; Paper~VI; Epilogue). \emph{Two} chains lead from the
decisions to Hilbert space, and they must not be conflated. The
\emph{discovery} chain --- how it actually happened --- ran spectral geometry
$\to$ EML $\to$ Bohrification ($+$KS) $\to$ Sol\`er: each arrow a motivation,
none a proof; APP\_06 records it, correctly, as design history. The
\emph{logical} chain carries the weight, and each link bears an explicit
strength tag:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{[DESIGN]} n/ commits to: negation $!$ as an orthocomplement
  (an \textbf{orthomodular} observation logic); fingerprints that carry phase
  (interference / nearness-before-fetch --- the smell-search requirement); and
  observation that is contextual (non-trivial) with \emph{continuous}
  dynamics. These are design axioms --- stated, not derived. (Deriving
  orthomodularity itself, e.g.\ from the $\mathcal Q\dashv\mathcal B$
  adjunction, is open: RESEARCH\_FRONTIER item~5.)
  \item \textbf{[CONDITION]} the semantic lattice is an infinite-dimensional
  orthomodular space admitting an infinite orthonormal sequence --- supplied,
  plausibly but not provenly, by the CAID Laplacian spectrum (APP\_06 \S3.1
  note). Same evidence-grade as the SPEC\_17 modeling identification of \S4.
  \item \textbf{[THEOREM --- Sol\`er 1995]} under that condition, the scalar
  field lies in $\{\RR,\C,\HH\}$.
  \item \textbf{[THEOREM --- Paper VI]} within that classification, the design
  axioms select $\C$ by a pincer. $\RR$: its phase group $O(1)\cong\Z/2$ is
  discrete --- it cannot carry continuous evolution (the continuity
  constraint). $\HH$: its phase group $Sp(1)\cong SU(2)$ is perfect, so
  $\mathrm{Hom}(SU(2),U(1))=0$ and the $d_2$ transgression that realizes
  Kochen--Specker contextuality is annihilated (the abelian constraint). Only
  $U(1)$ survives; hence $\C$.
  \item \textbf{[ECHO --- conjectural; item 4]} \textbf{EML.} n/ asks its
  mathematical layer to be \emph{semantically closed} --- generated from a
  single primitive (the ``math LUCA'' of SPEC\_09) rather than postulating
  each elementary function --- and one operator
  $\mathrm{eml}(x,y)=\exp(x)-\ln(y)$ achieves this closure. Over $\C$,
  $\ln(-1)=i\pi$ is a well-defined $U(1)$ ambiguity; over $\HH$ the logarithm
  admits a continuous $S^2$ of branches, breaking single-valued computation;
  over $\RR$ the closure fails. Paper~VI itself files this as a ``suggestive
  computational echo'' whose equivalence to the $d_2$ mechanism is left as
  future work: independent supporting evidence, and the design story of the
  seed --- \emph{not} a load-bearing eliminator.
\end{itemize}
\begin{remark}
The two chains assign EML opposite roles, and that is the point: in the
discovery chain EML was the hinge (it is what suggested $\C$); in the logical
chain it is an echo, and the eliminations are Paper~VI's theorems. Earlier
drafts of this section blended the chains --- the discovery chain's order of
importance wearing the logical chain's rhetoric. The dependency, not the
chronology, is what ``forced'' refers to: forced \emph{modulo} the two flagged
inputs ([DESIGN], [CONDITION]) --- the same conditional shape as the $n=4$
claim of \S4.
\end{remark}
Quantum structure is non-commutative. \textbf{Bohrification} (Paper~II; Epilogue)
restores readability by observing the non-commutative ontology through a family
of commutative subalgebras (contexts); the projection operator $P_A$ recovered
from those local sections \emph{is} the CAID. The cost is named by
Kochen--Specker (Paper~III): there is \emph{no} observer-independent global
section, so an observer is necessarily bound to a perspective and a horizon. The
hard question both decisions inherit:

\begin{quote}
\emph{In a forced-quantum, contextual universe, how badly does content-addressing
by observation fail to glue --- and is that failure bounded?}
\end{quote}

\section{From semantics to symplectic geometry (the bridge)}
% =====================================================================

The papers work in the \emph{characteristic-2 shadow} of the spec's $\C$-Hilbert
geometry: the stabilizer / Pauli picture $V=\F_2^{2n}$. This is not a loss of rigor
but a \emph{lossless} move for the question at hand. The contextuality obstructions
(KS, Mermin) are \emph{stabilizer} phenomena, and the Pauli group modulo phase is
\emph{exactly} $V=\F_2^{2n}$ --- with the symplectic form $\omega$ recording
commutation and the quadratic refinement $q$ recording the $\pm1,\pm i$ phases. The
continuous $\C$-amplitudes play no part in the obstruction, so the shadow discards
the geometry it does not need and keeps \emph{all} of the contextuality. The
dictionary that carries the spec-side question into it:

\paragraph{A worked row: ``context'' $=$ Lagrangian.} On the symplectic side, a
\emph{Lagrangian} is a maximal isotropic subspace $L\subset V=\F_2^{2n}$:
$\dim L=n$ and $\omega|_{L}\equiv0$. For $3$ qubits ($V=\F_2^6$, identifying
$v=(x,z)$ with the Pauli operator $X^xZ^z$ up to phase), two operators commute iff
$\omega(u,v)=0$, so a Lagrangian is a maximal set of $n=3$ independent
mutually-commuting Paulis --- e.g.\ $\langle X_1,X_2,X_3\rangle$ or
$\langle Z_1,Z_2,Z_3\rangle$ --- exactly a stabilizer context. On the n/ side this
is a MASA / observation context (SPEC\_13 \S7): a maximal set of Combos observable
together, i.e.\ whose meet does not collapse. Schematically,
\begin{verbatim}
  a & b    ;; co-observable (commute): meet refines -> one context
  a & c    ;; incompatible (anticommute): collapses to _|_ -> #split
\end{verbatim}
``same context'' (n/) $=$ ``commute'' ($\omega=0$) $=$ ``share a Lagrangian'' ---
the single identification that makes the rest of the dictionary run.

Two further points fix the reading.

\textbf{Contexts are observation frames, and gluing is the whole question.} A
Lagrangian is a maximal set of observables seen at once; the nerve records how
frames overlap. A global section would be the observer-independent value table KS
forbids; everything downstream measures its \emph{failure}.

\textbf{$\hbarn$ is the depth of that failure, and the ladder computes it.} The
spec defines $\hbarn$ as the number of commutative contexts needed to describe a
value (APP\_06 \S6.4): $\approx0$ when one context suffices (classical), finite
when several must be superposed. The normalization ($\hbarn\approx0$ at one
context) fixes the bookkeeping: $\hbarn$ counts the contexts \emph{beyond} the
first, so an arity-$a$ datum --- spanning $a$ contexts, resonant on the clique
$K_{a+1}$ --- has $\hbarn=a-1$, its cohomological \emph{degree}. This
($\hbarn=$ degree $=$ arity$\,-1$ $=$ clique$\,-2$) is the one convention used
throughout, in particular in the spectrum table of \S\ref{sec:ladder}; the
series turns it into a discrete spectrum.

\begin{remark}
The spec's published CAID (per \texttt{integration\_map.md}) is anchored only to
Papers~I--VI; it predates the symplectic ($\Sp(2n,\F_2)$) language of
Papers~VII--XXII. The dictionary row ``CAID $=$ the $\omega/q$-Gram fingerprint''
is therefore the \emph{extension} of CAID into that later language, not a
restatement of the current spec --- now reconciled: SPEC\_13 \S1.3 records this
symplectic characterization of CAID (the physical envelope, REAL\_03, still uses
the complex spectrum; the symplectic fingerprint is its mathematical content).
\end{remark}

The base case is Bohrification itself (Paper~II): the original, $1$-truncated
bridge presenting a non-commutative structure by the diagram of its commutative
contexts. n/ computes the \emph{higher} gluing obstructions Bohrification left
implicit --- the series is, in one line, the \emph{cohomological completion of
Bohrification}, and the completion \emph{terminates}:

\begin{theorem}[Resonance ceiling; Paper~XXII]
For symmetric (bilinear, Pauli) data the obstruction tower stops at $H^3$: the
canonical degree-4 assembly is exact ($c=\delta a$), and there is no genuine
arity-5 invariant. Hence $\hbarn^{\mathrm{eff}}$ has a finite, third-order
ceiling: no value ever requires its contextuality to be witnessed above $H^3$.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Why no arity-5 rung (the intuition).} The anticommutation datum
$\omega(v_{ij},v_{kl})$ is indexed by four Lagrangian labels $\{i,j,k,l\}$ (a
disjoint pair of pairs): a 4-index datum, hence a degree-3 cochain no matter how
many contexts are present. On $K_6$ (degree 4) it is therefore \emph{sub-top}, so
the assembled degree-4 cochain is forced to be the coboundary of that 4-index data
($c=\delta a$, exact) --- never a primary $H^4$ class. A genuine arity-5
obstruction would need a true 5-index invariant, which the \emph{bilinear} form
$\omega$ cannot supply; that is the whole content of the ceiling.

This is the spec's payoff made rigorous \emph{without} the Lohmiller--Slotine
route, whose ``exact'' reconstruction is challenged (Vattay's comment shows it is
semiclassical/WKB; APP\_06 \S6 caveat). The rigorous anchor for $\hbarn$ is
Bohrification plus this series.

\section{\texorpdfstring{The master dichotomy: $n=4$, and holography}{The master dichotomy: n=4, and holography}}
% =====================================================================

Beneath the ceiling sits the sharpest result of the ladder.

\begin{theorem}[Master dichotomy; Papers~XVIII, XXI]
The top ($H^3$) obstruction vanishes universally,
\[
   [\,n_a\,]=[\delta\mu]=0 \ \text{for all configurations} \iff n=4 .
\]
For $n=3$ it vanishes only partially; for $n=4$ it is identically trivial; for
$n\ge5$ it is alive, carried by the \emph{modulus} $\rad(\omega|_W)$ --- the order
parameter that distinguishes the $n\ge5$ strata (Papers~XIX, XX).
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Holographic reading (Paper~XIX; APP\_07 \S6).} The $n\ge5$ modulus reads
as a \emph{holographic} statement: the bulk ($H^3$) class is not recovered from the
boundary (arity-$\le4$) data --- the modulus $\rad(\omega|_W)$ is an order parameter
that no arity-$\le4$ invariant tested so far reconstructs (Paper~XIX). Stated with
the program's standing discipline, this is ``the tested boundary-level family is
blind,'' not ``no boundary invariant can exist''; $H^3$ is the first level where
this boundary-to-bulk gap shows up. The spec reads this (APP\_07 \S6) as why n/ is
observation-centric rather than global-truth: no ``god's-eye view'' reconstructs the
bulk from local ($\le4$-context) certificates.

\paragraph{From holographic failure to the choice of $n=4$.} The holographic
deficit has a self-referential bite. At $n\ge5$ the bulk ($H^3$) is alive yet not
reconstructable from $\le4$-context data; so an $n\ge5$ system cannot \emph{fully
understand} itself from its own observable data, and coherent self-description
fails above $n=4$. This is why, when n/ attempts fully coherent self-description and
self-evolution (the Ouroboros loop), it lands at $n=4$: the one dimension with
$[n_a]=0$.

\emph{One caveat, to head off a conflation.} ``Boundary data is insufficient'' is
\textbf{not} itself the discriminator between $n=4$ and $n\ge5$: it holds at $n=4$
too --- there the relation code is entirely global ($\dim R_4=0$) and boundary data
is just as incomplete, yet $[n_a]=0$ regardless. What actually makes $n=4$
obstruction-free is the dimensional coincidence $2n=8$ underlying the B1/B2 squeeze
(Paper~XVIII), \emph{not} boundary-insufficiency. So the holographic deficit is the
\emph{setting} in which self-description is hard at every $n$; the $2n=8$ squeeze is
the \emph{reason} exactly $n=4$ escapes. (Reading ``boundary-insufficient $\Rightarrow$
bulk hidden'' as the cause would repeat, at the narrative level, the
expressibility-vs-nonvanishing conflation the program guards against.)

\paragraph{Two roles, two dimensional behaviors.} What does $n=4$ mean for n/?
The answer turns on a distinction between the engine and the spec's self-evolution.

\emph{The Ouroboros engine does \textbf{not} fix $n$.} The discovery/runtime engine
(CAID $+$ LADD) must \emph{recognize} obstruction classes at every level: APP\_07
maps the ladder onto distributed consensus --- $H^1$ to CAP / network partition,
$H^2$ to FLP impossibility, $H^3$ to Byzantine non-associativity, $H^4$ to Sybil
identity-forgery (the reason ORDER\_00 exists). An engine that handles only one
dimension could not classify these. So the engine is \emph{dimension-agnostic};
it spans the whole ladder at whatever $n$ a configuration presents.

\emph{The Ouroboros self-evolution \textbf{does} fix $n=4$.} The reflexive loop
--- n/ using its own lattice to describe and evolve itself, the ``truth integral''
on its own \texttt{\%rules} (SPEC\_17 \S4) --- is \emph{coherent self-representation}.
For that loop to close without obstruction is exactly $[n_a]=0$, i.e.\ $n=4$. At
$n=4$ the third level of the $\hbarn$ spectrum is always empty: no
self-description ever forces a Borromean ($H^3$) obstruction, so the
otherwise-bottomless tower of self-observation closes coherently at finite degree
--- at, and only at, one dimension. This is the same statement as the program's
founding intuition (\emph{why\_the\_ladder}: the $\infty$-Yoneda self-embedding is
free at $n=4$).

\paragraph{Why self-coherence \emph{is} a cohomology class.} ``The loop closes''
sounds informal; it is not. A coherent self-description is a \emph{global section}
of the self-observation nerve --- a single assignment, consistent across every
context, of how the structure sees itself. The obstruction to such a global
section is, by definition, a cohomology class (the \v{C}ech/sheaf obstruction).
\emph{Given that the self-observation nerve instantiates the framework's
$K_5/\Sp(2n,\F_2)$ data} (the spec's correspondence, SPEC\_17 \S4.3 --- see the
caveat below), the top such class is $[n_a]\in H^3$, and ``self-description closes
coherently'' is then \emph{literally} ``$[n_a]=0$,'' which the master theorem makes
$n=4$. (This is the same move as everywhere in the program: a would-be global
truth, obstructed by a class in $H^*$ --- here applied to the structure's view of
\emph{itself}.)

\paragraph{[Modeling identification] what this hinges on.} The step that the
Ouroboros loop's self-observation nerve \emph{is} the specific $K_5/\Sp(2n,\F_2)$
configuration whose $H^3$ class the series computes --- not merely an obstruction
``of the same shape,'' but the very object one feeds to the Papers~XVIII/XXI
machine, for some $n$ tied to the loop's own architecture --- is the spec's

\section{The two faces are one}
\label{sec:onearena}
% =====================================================================

The specification and the series are two views of one claim.

\textbf{``Discovery, not invention'' is realism about the observation-structure,
not about values.} Kochen--Specker forbids an observer-independent table of
values; so what is discovered-not-invented is not values but the \emph{structure
of observation} --- contexts, nerve, obstruction tower. The realism survives by
being relocated from values to observation-structure, and the firewall that keeps
this honest is that every claim of ``essence'' is a class in $H^*$.

\textbf{Yoneda appears twice, and $n=4$ belongs to the second.} CAID is Yoneda at
the level of identity (an object is its relations / its $P_A$-fingerprint), and it
is dimension-agnostic --- the engine addresses objects at any $n$. The
self-evolution protocol (Ouroboros / SPEC\_17) is Yoneda made \emph{reflexive}
(the structure describing and evolving itself). The master theorem speaks to this
second, reflexive Yoneda: self-description closes coherently at exactly $n=4$.
Identity-by-relation spans the ladder; self-representation selects the dimension.

\textbf{One degree, every engineering layer.} The same cohomological degree
surfaces as different phenomena up the engineering stack (the $L_r\times E_r$
matrix, SPEC\_00): an $H^2$ obstruction is a type conflict at the library layer,
a compute-horizon failure at runtime, FLP impossibility at the network layer, a
governance contradiction at the trust layer. The mathematics is layer-independent;
the manifestation is not.

\textbf{No bottom, yet a ceiling --- and they do not contradict.} A reader may ask:
\S\ref{sec:ladder} proves a \emph{ceiling} at $H^3$, yet \S\ref{sec:frontier}
(item~23) says the bridge \emph{never finitely closes} --- does the tower have a
lid or not? Both, of different things:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{The abstract machine has no lid.} The LHS spectral sequence with
  Kudo's cross-step ($\tau(\Sq^i x)=\Sq^i\tau(x)$) is a bottomless spiral:
  generators at degrees $\{2,3,5,9,\dots\}$, climbable forever. The universe
  \emph{permits} infinite self-reference --- this is the $\infty$ in $\infty$-Yoneda
  ($f^*\omega=0$ makes the map secondary, so fixing one frame only spawns the next
  degree: $\text{Bohr}\to\mu\to n_a\to\cdots$).
  \item \emph{The Pauli content has a lid.} But the data n/ actually lives on is
  \emph{bilinear} (Pauli), and its arity is exhausted at $4$: it resonates only
  through $H^3$. The next abstract generator (degree $5$ $=$ arity $6$) would need
  non-bilinear data the framework does not carry. So the \emph{realized}
  obstruction truncates at $H^3$ (Paper~XXII), even though the machine continues.
  \item \emph{And even the realized rungs are built infinitely.} Item~23: the
  explicit cochain for each rung is a secondary operation with no finite formula.
\end{itemize}
So: the machine is infinite, the Pauli-realized height is finite ($H^3$), and the
rung-construction is $\infty$-coherent. ``Observation has no bottom; the Pauli
ceiling and $n=4$ are where it closes.''

\section{The honest frontier}
\label{sec:frontier}
% =====================================================================

A whitepaper ends with what is not settled, at the right altitude.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Is the ceiling exotic-proof? (item 21) --- reduced, no longer an
  open search.} Paper~XXII shows the \emph{natural} obstruction stops at $H^3$.
  Whether an \emph{exotic} arity-5 invariant could furnish a new $H^4$ obstruction
  for $n\ge5$ is \emph{reduced} from an unparametrized search to: Witt's theorem
  $+$ polarization $+$ a \emph{single} naturality/representability condition $+$
  verified finite computations --- the unique degree-4 ambient invariant being the
  decomposable $q^2$ (so no exotic). That one condition --- genuine obstruction $=$
  a natural, pointwise-cohomological class --- is itself grounded two independent
  ways (phase-blindness of the over-counters; $H^4(BV)^{GL}=0$) and is, operationally,
  just the statement that contextuality is the standard value-assignment obstruction.
  \item \textbf{The explicit bridge never finitely closes (item 23) --- closed
  modulo Kudo.} The identification $n_a=\langle\Sq^1\omega,[K_5]\rangle$ holds; its
  join is a \emph{secondary} operation (Kudo transgression), so there is no finite
  cochain formula. The logical ground \emph{does} bottom out --- at the proven
  theorems of Kudo and Quillen --- so the bridge is closed \emph{modulo} those
  cited theorems; only the explicit realization is rebuilt at every degree, which
  is the $\infty$-coherence of self-representation, not a gap. (This is also the
  comparison map --- between Paper~IV's $16$-cell / \v{C}ech $H^3$ and Paper~XX's
  Maslov--Wall $H^3$, the same class in two languages --- that the spec's $H^3$
  entries await before the $16$-cell / compute-horizon identifications can be made
  drop-in.)
  \item \textbf{The forcing chain's hinges (items 4, 5) --- conditional, and
  flagged.} ``Forced into Hilbert space'' (\S1) is a theorem modulo two flagged
  inputs: the \textbf{[CONDITION]} that the semantic lattice satisfies
  Sol\`er's hypotheses (the infinite orthonormal sequence from the CAID
  spectral geometry), and the \textbf{[DESIGN]} axioms (orthomodular negation;
  continuous, contextual observation) --- deriving orthomodularity itself
  being the categorical residue (item~5). The EML selection is an echo, not a
  pillar (item~4): its equivalence to the $d_2$ mechanism is open, and nothing
  load-bearing rests on it.
\end{itemize}

The first two open ends are the same kind: the perpetual, higher-coherence
re-building of the one bridge --- that it never finitely finishes is the
mathematical shape of self-representation, not an incompleteness of the
program. The third is ordinary in kind: foundations stated as hypotheses,
awaiting discharge --- and flagged until then.
