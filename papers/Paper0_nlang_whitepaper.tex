\begin{abstract}
n/ is a semantic operating system built on a stance: meaning is
\emph{discovered, not invented}; the programmer is an \emph{observer}. Two
engineering decisions follow, and the n/ paper series exists to give each a
mathematical backing. First, identity is content-addressable by \emph{relation}:
a subspace is named by the spectral fingerprint of its projection operator $P_A$
(the \textbf{CAID}, Content Addressable Identifier; Yoneda's principle, an object
is its relations). Second, navigation is by \emph{geometric gravity} (``smell
search''), which demands fingerprints carry \emph{phase}. The second requirement
is not free: an orthomodular observation logic (Sol\`er) restricts the scalar
field to $\{\RR,\C,\HH\}$, and the EML completeness result selects $\C$ --- so n/
is \emph{forced} into Hilbert space; quantization is geometric necessity, not
style. Quantum structure is non-commutative, so observation proceeds through
commutative \emph{contexts} (Bohrification), and Kochen--Specker then guarantees
there is no observer-independent global section. The obstruction-ladder series
computes the \emph{exact} structure and \emph{bound} of that contextuality; the
specification itself reads its central output (APP\_06 \S6.5) as the spectrum of
$\hbarn$, the number of trust-contexts needed to describe a value: the
\emph{resonance tower} is that spectrum, it is \emph{discrete}, and it has a
\emph{ceiling at $H^3$} (Paper~XXII). Beneath the ceiling, the top class vanishes
universally if and only if $n=4$ (Paper~XXI). The specification poses the
question; the papers answer it. This document is the bridge between them ---
and, like every result it summarizes, it is itself a bridge.
\end{abstract}

\section{Two engineering decisions (the spec side)}
% =====================================================================

The specification opens with a stance, not a feature list: \emph{writing programs
is discovery, not invention}. The programmer is an \emph{observer} of structure
that is already there. Two design decisions follow; the paper series is their
backing.

\paragraph{Decision 1 --- identity by relation (CAID).}
A subspace's identity is not its name but its projection feature in Hilbert space
(SPEC\_13 \S1). The \textbf{CAID} (Content Addressable Identifier) is the
intrinsic identifier: the spectral-geometric fingerprint of the projection
operator $P_A$, carrying both a collision-resistant particle digest and a
wave-like spectral sketch. Its justification is \emph{Yoneda's principle}: an
object is determined by its relations; the CAID encapsulates the interference of
$P_A$ with every other operator. Names (``observation aliases'') are relative
observer labels; the CAID is the absolute geometric coordinate.

\paragraph{Decision 2 --- navigation by geometric gravity (smell search).}
The genuine root of the program is a spectral-geometry problem: find a
permutation-invariant, collision-resistant fingerprint of a lattice element that
also decides geometric \emph{inclusion} --- via the Laplacian spectrum of its
Hasse diagram, with Weyl-law stability and Cauchy interlacing (the original
``smell search'' open problem). Discovery is then \emph{gravity collapse}:
declaring a dependency applies a positioning constraint and the engine navigates
along the gradient of semantic gravity (SPEC\_13 \S2, \S6). For two fingerprints
to attract --- to have a usable \emph{distance} before download --- the
fingerprint must carry \emph{phase}, not just a real scalar.

\paragraph{Why the second decision forces quantum geometry.}
Phase is exactly what a real scalar cannot supply, and the consequences cascade
(APP\_06 \S2--\S4; Paper~VI; Epilogue). Logically:
\begin{itemize}
  \item n/'s negation $!$ makes the logic an \textbf{orthomodular lattice};
  \textbf{Sol\`er's theorem} then restricts its scalar field to
  $\{\RR,\C,\HH\}$.
  \item \textbf{EML completeness} --- a single operator
  $\mathrm{eml}(x,y)=\exp(x)-\ln(y)$ self-bootstraps the elementary functions,
  but is complete only over $\C$ --- selects $\C$ (and supplies the phase smell
  search needs).
  \item Hence n/ is \emph{forced} into Hilbert space: ``quantization is not a
  style but the geometric necessity of a lattice meeting EML completeness.''
\end{itemize}
\begin{remark}
The order above is the logical reconstruction the spec now uses; the historical
discovery order ran spectral geometry $\to$ EML $\to$ Bohrification ($+$KS) $\to$
Sol\`er. The dependency, not the chronology, is what matters here.
\end{remark}
Quantum structure is non-commutative. \textbf{Bohrification} (Paper~II; Epilogue)
restores readability by observing the non-commutative ontology through a family
of commutative subalgebras (contexts); the projection operator $P_A$ recovered
from those local sections \emph{is} the CAID. The cost is named by
Kochen--Specker (Paper~III): there is \emph{no} observer-independent global
section, so an observer is necessarily bound to a perspective and a horizon. The
hard question both decisions inherit:

\begin{quote}
\emph{In a forced-quantum, contextual universe, how badly does content-addressing
by observation fail to glue --- and is that failure bounded?}
\end{quote}

Specific entries are anchored by individual papers: the complex-spectrum
sign/phase of CAID~v2 is the $\beta$/quadratic-refinement structure (Paper~XI);

$\Sp(6,\F_2)$ on $\C^8$ (Paper~XV). Two points fix the rest.

\textbf{Contexts are observation frames, and gluing is the whole question.} A
Lagrangian is a maximal set of observables seen at once; the nerve records how
frames overlap. A global section would be the observer-independent value table KS
forbids; everything downstream measures its \emph{failure}.

\textbf{$\hbarn$ is the depth of that failure, and the ladder computes it.} The
spec defines $\hbarn$ as the number of commutative contexts needed to describe a
value (APP\_06 \S6.4): $\approx0$ when one context suffices (classical), finite
when several must be superposed. Through the dictionary this is the obstruction's
\emph{arity}, which the series turns into a discrete spectrum.

\section{The ladder is the spectrum of $\hbarn$}
\label{sec:ladder}
% =====================================================================

The specification already states the bridge (APP\_06 \S6.5): the \emph{resonance
tower} of Paper~XXII \emph{is} the discrete spectrum of $\hbarn$. The
arity-resonance principle --- an arity-$a$ datum is an $(a{-}1)$-cochain that
resonates on the clique $K_{a+1}$ and yields an $H^{a-1}$ obstruction --- makes
``how many contexts describe a value'' a cohomological degree:

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabular}{p{0.07\linewidth} p{0.07\linewidth} p{0.50\linewidth} p{0.22\linewidth}}
\toprule
$\hbarn$ & degree & obstruction (clique) & paper(s) \\
\midrule
1 & $H^1$ & geometric phase / holonomy (arity 2, $K_3$) & I \\

           Mermin square $K_{3,3}$ (family B) & III, X, XIII, XVII, XVIII \\
3 & $H^3$ & Borromean / Byzantine (arity 4, $K_5$ pentagram); $n_a=\Sq^1\omega$
        & IV, XX \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{ceiling}} &
  no genuine arity-5 rung; $c=\delta a$ exact; clique criterion $\min(\omega-2,3)$
        & XXII \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The base case is Bohrification itself (Paper~II): the original, $1$-truncated
bridge presenting a non-commutative structure by the diagram of its commutative
contexts. n/ computes the \emph{higher} gluing obstructions Bohrification left
implicit --- the series is, in one line, the \emph{cohomological completion of
Bohrification}, and the completion \emph{terminates}:

\begin{theorem}[Resonance ceiling; Paper~XXII]
For symmetric (bilinear, Pauli) data the obstruction tower stops at $H^3$: the
canonical degree-4 assembly is exact ($c=\delta a$), and there is no genuine
arity-5 invariant. Hence $\hbarn^{\mathrm{eff}}$ has a finite, third-order
ceiling: no value ever requires its contextuality to be witnessed above $H^3$.
\end{theorem}

This is the spec's payoff made rigorous \emph{without} the Lohmiller--Slotine
route, whose ``exact'' reconstruction is challenged (Vattay's comment shows it is
semiclassical/WKB; APP\_06 \S6 caveat). The rigorous anchor for $\hbarn$ is
Bohrification plus this series.

\section{The master dichotomy: $n=4$, and holography}
% =====================================================================

Beneath the ceiling sits the sharpest result of the ladder.

\begin{theorem}[Master dichotomy; Papers~XVIII, XXI]
The top ($H^3$) obstruction vanishes universally,
\[
   [\,n_a\,]=[\delta\mu]=0 \ \text{for all configurations} \iff n=4 .
\]
For $n=3$ it vanishes only partially; for $n=4$ it is identically trivial; for
$n\ge5$ it is alive, carried by the modulus $\rad(\omega|_W)$ (Papers~XIX, XX).
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Holographic reading (Paper~XIX).} The $n\ge5$ modulus is a precise
\emph{holographic} statement: the bulk ($H^3$) class \emph{cannot} be
reconstructed from the boundary (arity-$\le4$) data. The obstruction ladder
measures exactly this boundary-to-bulk reconstruction failure, and $H^3$ is the
first level where boundary data is in principle insufficient.

\paragraph{$\hbarn$ reading.} At $n=4$ the third level of the spectrum is always
empty --- no value forces Borromean ($H^3$) contextuality --- so self-observation
is free of obstruction up to the ceiling. This is the precise sense in which
$n=4$ is distinguished: the otherwise-bottomless tower of self-observation closes
coherently at finite degree at exactly one dimension.

\section{The two faces are one}
\label{sec:onearena}
% =====================================================================

The specification and the series are two views of one claim.

\textbf{``Discovery, not invention'' is realism about the observation-structure,
not about values.} Kochen--Specker forbids an observer-independent table of
values; so what is discovered-not-invented is not values but the \emph{structure
of observation} --- contexts, nerve, obstruction tower. The realism survives by
being relocated from values to observation-structure, and the firewall that keeps
this honest is that every claim of ``essence'' is a class in $H^*$.

\textbf{Yoneda appears twice, and they are the same arrow.} CAID is Yoneda at the
level of identity (an object is its relations / its $P_A$-fingerprint); the
self-discovery protocol (Ouroboros / OODP) is Yoneda made reflexive (the universe
addressing its own contents). The obstruction tower measures when that reflexive
self-address is coherent.

\textbf{One degree, every engineering layer.} The same cohomological degree
surfaces as different phenomena up the engineering stack (the $L_r\times E_r$
matrix, SPEC\_00): an $H^2$ obstruction is a type conflict at the library layer,
a compute-horizon failure at runtime, FLP impossibility at the network layer, a
governance contradiction at the trust layer. The mathematics is layer-independent;
the manifestation is not.

\textbf{Observation has no bottom; the ceiling and $n=4$ are where it closes.}
Because the self-representation map is secondary (its primary form vanishes,
$f^*\omega=0$), fixing one global frame only spawns the next degree:
$\text{Bohr}\to\mu\to n_a\to\cdots$. This bottomless reflexivity is the honest
face of the $\infty$ in $\infty$-Yoneda --- ``building bridges forever'' is the
signature of an $\infty$-categorical self-embedding, where the bridge \emph{is}
the content. The series shows the tower nonetheless terminates twice: a universal
ceiling at $H^3$ (Paper~XXII), and a unique dimension $n=4$ where even the top
rung is always trivial.

\section{The honest frontier}
% =====================================================================

A whitepaper ends with what is not settled, at the right altitude.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Is the ceiling exotic-proof? (item 21).} Paper~XXII shows the
  \emph{natural} obstruction stops at $H^3$. Whether an \emph{exotic} arity-5
  invariant could furnish a new $H^4$ obstruction for $n\ge5$ is \emph{reduced},
  not closed: to ``Witt + polarization + a representability condition + finite
  verified computations,'' the unique degree-4 ambient invariant being the
  decomposable $q^2$. The single residual is a naturality/representability
  condition --- itself the statement that the framework's obstruction is the
  standard contextuality (value-assignment) obstruction.
  \item \textbf{The explicit bridge never finitely closes (item 23).} The
  identification $n_a=\langle\Sq^1\omega,[K_5]\rangle$ holds, but its join is a
  \emph{secondary} operation (Kudo transgression): no finite cochain formula. The
  logical ground does bottom out --- at the proven theorems of Kudo and Quillen
  --- but the explicit realization is rebuilt at every degree. This is the
  $\infty$-coherence of self-representation, not a gap. (This is also the
  \emph{Direction-D} comparison map that the spec's $H^3$ entries await before
  the $16$-cell / compute-horizon identifications can be made drop-in.)
\end{itemize}

Both open ends are the same kind: the perpetual, higher-coherence re-building of
the one bridge. That it never finitely finishes is the mathematical shape of
self-representation, not an incompleteness of the program.
