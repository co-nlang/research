## The resonance (✓ as philosophy)

The amplituhedron (Arkani-Hamed–Trnka 2013) computes the S-matrix — the *observable* —
as the canonical form of a **positive geometry**, with **locality and unitarity
emergent**, not assumed. The Feynman-diagram "process" is a non-fundamental artifact.
That is `n/`'s `why_the_ladder` stance one domain over: the **observation/record is
primary**, and the global mechanistic description (a god's-eye truth assignment, the
"bulk process") is emergent-or-obstructed. Both demote the in-between machinery in
favour of a geometry of observables.

Both also share three ingredients: **Grassmannian + boundary stratification +
cohomological canonical form** (dlog forms = cohomology; cf. twisted-cohomology /
intersection-theory for Feynman integrals, Mizera et al.). Each instantiates
"physical/semantic content = a cohomology class on a combinatorial geometry."

## The useful part: they are near-*duals*

- **Amplituhedron = the geometry where gluing *succeeds*.** Positivity forces the
  canonical form to exist; singularities sit only on boundaries (= factorization).
  No obstruction — the success story.
- **Obstruction ladder = the geometry of how gluing *fails*.** Over $\mathbb F_2$,
  no positivity; the entire content is the $H^k$ measure of the failure to glue.

This maps onto **APP_07 §6** (holographic note): the amplituhedron is the canonical
**"boundary determines bulk"** success (emergent locality from boundary positivity);
the modulus theorem (Paper XIX) is **"boundary *under*determines bulk"** (the $H^3$
class invisible to $\le4$-arity boundary data). **The amplituhedron is the positive
control for our holographic framing** — that is the one place it actively earns its
keep.

## The one well-posed (but very, very distant) technical hook

Not "two analogies" — two structures on the *same* twistor space:

- **Paper VIII:** $\Phi = \ell\circ\tau\circ\iota^*$ maps the **$K_{3,3}$ nerve →
  $\mathbb{CP}^3$** (Penrose twistor space), carrying a **$\mathbb Z/2$-gerbe $\in
  H^2(\mathbb{CP}^3)$** (the googly / $H^2$ obstruction, Paper VII).
- **Amplituhedron:** maps the **positive Grassmannian → momentum-twistor
  $\mathbb{CP}^3$** ($\mathbb P^{3|4}$, Hodges variables), carrying a **positive
  geometry / canonical form**.

> **The well-posed question:** on the *same* twistor $\mathbb{CP}^3$, is there a
> rigorous comparison map relating Paper VIII's $H^2$-gerbe structure to the
> amplituhedron's positive-geometry / canonical-form structure?

If yes, it stops being analogy. This is XXIII++-level and may be a coincidence.

## Two deflations (honesty)

1. **$\mathbb{CP}^3$ is twistor space *because of 4d*** — so part of "both land in
   $\mathbb{CP}^3$" is a dimensional coincidence. Worse, Paper VIII's $\mathbb{CP}^3$
   arrives via the 3-qubit chain $\mathrm{GL}(3,\mathbb F_2)\hookrightarrow
   \mathrm{PSp}(6,\mathbb F_2)\to$ twistor (Paper IX). It uses a genuine twisted
   Penrose transform, but its *entrance* is combinatorial — so it is plausibly a
   **representation-theoretic echo** of twistor geometry, not 4d momentum space.
2. **Positivity is the soul of the amplituhedron, and $\mathbb F_2$ has no order.**
   `Gr_{≥0}` lives over $\mathbb R$; there is no honest "positive Grassmannian over
   $\mathbb F_2$." The frameworks live in different number systems *on purpose*
   ($\mathbb F_2$ = the symplectic/Pauli substrate; $\mathbb R_{≥0}$ = positivity).

The hook (twistor) and the gap (positivity / $\mathbb F_2$) are two faces of one wall:
the framework's twistor side comes *from* $\mathbb F_2$, which is exactly where
positivity cannot follow. If any face of this framework has a positive-geometry shadow
at all, it is on the **$\mathbb C$-lift** — Paper XV's Weil/metaplectic representation,
the only place the $\mathbb F_2$ data becomes a genuine complex Hilbert space — not
downstairs.
